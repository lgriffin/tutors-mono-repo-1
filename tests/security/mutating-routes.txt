# Every route that changes state, and who may call it (runway tier M; the seed
# of tier F's authorisation matrix). tests/security/security.test.ts fails when
# a +server.ts exports POST/PUT/PATCH/DELETE or a +page.server.ts declares an
# action that is not listed here, or when a listed route disappears.
# The container smoke test posts a cross-site form to each route and expects 403.
#
# Format: `<app> <METHOD> <route id> | who: <callers>`
# Add `| hook` for routes a hook mounts (discovery cannot see them).

time POST /api/sync | who: anyone who can reach the time app today; the handler has no authentication or role check (tier F must add one). It writes only what it fetches from Moodle, with the service_role key
reader POST /api/analytics | who: a signed-in student (verified identity (locals.actor), else 401); rows are written under the session's login (Rules 0071, 0072)
reader PUT /api/me | who: a signed-in user, for their own tutors-connect-users row; name, email and avatar come from the session
reader PATCH /api/me | who: a signed-in user, for their own sentiment and online status (Rule 0072)
reader PUT /api/profile | who: a signed-in user, for their own course-visit history
reader POST /api/courses/visit | who: anyone viewing a course; counts a visit to a course the server finds published, with title and credits from that course
reader POST /api/presence | who: a signed-in student who shares presence; stored under the session's login (Rule 0072)
reader PUT /api/locks | who: a signed-in educator of the course (enrollment.yaml educators or PRIVATE_TUTORS_ADMINS), else 403 (Rule 0065)
reader DELETE /api/locks | who: a signed-in educator of the course, else 403 (Rule 0065)
reader PUT /api/whiteboard | who: a signed-in user; a personal room is always the session's own (Rule 0068)
reader PUT /api/scorm | who: a signed-in learner, for their own SCORM state; stored under the session's login whatever the body names (Rule 0473)
reader PUT /api/playground | who: a signed-in student, for their own hand-in; student id and name come from the session (Rule 0475)
reader DELETE /api/playground | who: a signed-in student, for their own hand-in only; an educator withdraws nobody's (Rule 0475)
reader POST /api/errors | who: anyone, as the anon insert it replaces allowed, from the reader or an origin in PRIVATE_API_ALLOWED_ORIGINS; 16 KB, 20 entries, 30 reports a minute per address; the student is the session's (Rule 0472)
reader POST /api/live/watch | who: anyone, as the anon realtime_watch it replaces allowed, from the reader or an origin in PRIVATE_API_ALLOWED_ORIGINS (Tutors Live); the topic must be a Live activity topic (Rule 0472)
reader POST /api/auth/sign-in/social | hook | who: anyone; GitHub only, Better Auth verifies origin and return URL
reader POST /api/auth/callback/github | hook | who: OAuth callback holder; Better Auth verifies signed state (GitHub normally uses GET)
reader POST /api/auth/sign-out | hook | who: session holder; Better Auth verifies origin and deletes browser cookies
reader POST /api/auth/get-session | hook | who: session holder; Better Auth verifies cookies and renews the session
reader POST /api/auth/update-user | hook | who: session holder; Better Auth verifies origin, adapter rejects githubId/login changes including null
reader POST /api/live/activity | who: anyone, as the anon key it replaces allowed; the body is validated (4 KB, LoRecord shape) and published only to watched topics within the day's Live budget (on Valkey, to the course's and the platform's activity topics, Rule 0481)
reader POST /api/realtime/publish | who: a signed-in reader tab (401 otherwise) from the reader's own pages (another Origin is 403); only a playground topic's `state` for the sender's own login or an educator's `watching`, 256 KB, 10 a second per sender on a topic (Rule 0484)
reader POST /api/gists | who: a signed-in student (the actor from the session); 403 in anonymous mode, 401 without an actor, 413 over 400 kB, 429 past 3 shares an hour; the body is validated (parseSnippetInput) and kept under the service key
reader POST /api/notices | who: a signed-in educator the course's published enrollment lists (requireEducator, fails closed); 429 past one broadcast a minute per course; the body is validated (parseBroadcastMessage) and published to the course's broadcast topic
time POST /api/auth/sign-in/social | hook | who: anyone; GitHub only, Better Auth verifies origin and return URL (the educator sign-in for the snippets page)
time POST /api/auth/callback/github | hook | who: OAuth callback holder; Better Auth verifies signed state
time POST /api/auth/sign-out | hook | who: session holder; Better Auth verifies origin and deletes browser cookies
time POST /api/auth/get-session | hook | who: session holder; Better Auth verifies cookies and renews the session
time POST /api/auth/update-user | hook | who: session holder; Better Auth verifies origin, adapter rejects githubId/login changes including null
live POST /api/live/events | who: anyone; the Tutors Live ingest endpoint is anonymous by design, so validation and a 64KB body limit are the only controls (tier F must add a rate limit)
